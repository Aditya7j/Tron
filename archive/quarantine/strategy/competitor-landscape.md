# Competitor Landscape

Four coffee businesses within a mile. Knowing precisely what each is good at is more useful than pretending we beat all of them.

Bluewater Coffee (two blocks). A regional chain, cheaper by about 15%, faster, open at 5:30am, and consistently mediocre. They win the pure commuter who wants speed. We do not compete on speed; we compete on the 8am customer who will wait four minutes, per [[Regulars and Community]].

Fathom Roasters (half a mile). The most respected roaster in the city, more expensive than us, wholesale-first, minimal café. They are the reason we sit second on price rather than first, per [[Pricing Strategy]], and they are the real competition for the accounts in [[Wholesale Accounts]].

The Hartwell Hotel café. Not a competitor — a customer. They buy 9kg a week from us.

Kiosk on Hawthorne. Seasonal, cash only, competes with the [[Farmers Market Booth]] on Saturdays and beats us on convenience, never on coffee.

Where we actually win: staff continuity, traceable sourcing like [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]], the pastry case from [[Pastry Partnership]], and being the shop people bring visitors to. That last one is the story in [[Local Press Outreach]].

Where we lose: price against Bluewater, roasting reputation against Fathom, hours against everyone. Hours are the cheapest of those to fix and would need the headcount in [[Hiring Pipeline]].
