# Staff Scheduling Rules

Labour is 37% of revenue in [[Monthly PnL Summary]] and it is the number that decides whether a month is good. It is also the number that, cut wrongly, destroys the thing customers actually pay for.

Hard rules
- Two on bar from 7 to 10 on weekdays. The rush between 7:10 and 8:45 in [[Daily Opening Checklist]] is 40% of weekday transactions and one person cannot hold it.
- Opening shift is always someone signed off under [[Barista Training Plan]], never a trainee alone.
- The [[Farmers Market Booth]] Saturday crew is additional headcount, never pulled off the café bar.
- Roast days are protected. Tuesday and Friday mornings the roaster is not also on bar, per [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Schedule published 10 days out. Changing a published shift requires asking, not telling.
- Nobody closes then opens.

Soft patterns that work: 2pm to close is one person plus prep; Mondays are thin and the right day for deep cleaning under [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]]; the pastry order in [[Pastry Partnership]] is set by the shift lead who saw the traffic.

Understaffing shows up within two weeks as slow service complaints in [[Customer Feedback Log]] and as turnover, which is far more expensive than the shift we saved — the argument in [[Payroll and Tips Policy]].
