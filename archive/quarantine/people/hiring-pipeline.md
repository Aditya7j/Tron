# Hiring Pipeline

We hire about four people a year to hold a team of nine. Two are replacements, which is a turnover rate we would like to halve.

Process, four steps. A short application, a twenty minute conversation, a paid two hour trial shift on the bar during a real but not peak period, then references. The trial shift decides almost everything.

What we look for, in order: warmth with strangers, composure under a queue, willingness to be corrected, and only then coffee experience. Experienced baristas with bad habits take longer to retrain than beginners take to train, a lesson that shaped the four weeks in [[Barista Training Plan]].

Where hires come from. Referrals from current staff, which produce the best retention and are worth a bonus under [[Payroll and Tips Policy]]. Regulars who ask, per [[Regulars and Community]]. A sign in the window. Never a job board, which produced twelve applications and no hires.

Timing. Hire before the summer cold brew season in [[Cold Brew Recipe]] and before an autumn launch in [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]], because a four week ramp means a September hire is useless in September. The roster constraints in [[Staff Scheduling Rules]] set the real deadline.

A roaster hire is a different search entirely and would be the first requirement of either [[Second Location Analysis]] or the wholesale growth in [[Wholesale Accounts]].
