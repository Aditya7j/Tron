# Regulars and Community

About 180 people account for a little over half of café revenue. They come in three to six times a week and they are the business.

Who they are. The 6:30 crowd, mostly trades and hospital shifts, drip and a morning bun, in and out in four minutes. The 8am commuters, milk drinks, the queue noted in [[Customer Feedback Log]]. The 10am freelancers, who stay two hours and want an outlet. The Saturday families, who buy bags and ask about the [[Farmers Market Booth]].

What keeps them. Their name, their order remembered, and the same face behind the bar — which makes staff turnover the single biggest risk to this group and the reason for [[Payroll and Tips Policy]] and [[Barista Training Plan]]. Learning twenty regulars by name is a week two task in the training plan.

Community things we do and will keep doing: a free monthly public cupping, promoted only in [[Email Newsletter Strategy]]; the community board by the door; hosting the neighbourhood association for the price of a carafe; the high school jazz trio in December.

Community things we tried and stopped: open mic night, which cost staff and attracted no coffee sales, and a punch card competition that felt cheap next to [[Loyalty Program]].

These are the people who make [[Second Location Analysis]] a hard question — they belong to this room, not to the brand.
