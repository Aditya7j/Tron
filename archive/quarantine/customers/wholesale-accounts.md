# Wholesale Accounts

Seven accounts, $12,600 a month, roughly 21% of revenue and the most stable line in [[Monthly PnL Summary]].

The book
- Ledger & Loom (bookshop café) — 18kg a week of [[House Blend Ember]], our largest and oldest account, found us at the [[Farmers Market Booth]].
- Third Rail Diner — 12kg a week, blend only, price sensitive, never complains.
- Hartwell Hotel — 9kg a week plus a decaf line, the account that demanded consistency and taught us to log every batch in the [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Two office contracts, 6kg a week combined, low touch, high margin.
- Two small cafés at 4kg each, both of which came from the coverage in [[Local Press Outreach]].

How it works. Roasted Tuesday, delivered Wednesday morning, net 15. Price ladder by volume, held in [[Pricing Strategy]]. Margin runs 52-58% cost per [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]], thinner than retail but with no labour attached beyond delivery.

What loses an account: inconsistency, not price. Two accounts churned in year two, both after we improvised the blend during a Colombia stockout — the exact failure recorded in [[Green Bean Inventory]].

Partners get a quarterly note on upcoming lots and price changes, kept separate from the consumer list in [[Email Newsletter Strategy]].

The canned cold brew from [[Cold Brew Recipe]] is the obvious next product for this channel.
