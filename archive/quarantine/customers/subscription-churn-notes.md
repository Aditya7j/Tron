# Subscription Churn Notes

410 active subscribers. 12oz or 2lb, every two or four weeks, shipped or collected. About $7,100 a month combined with retail bags in [[Monthly PnL Summary]].

Monthly churn is 6.5%, which means we replace half the book every year. Exit reasons, from cancellation replies:
- "Too much coffee" — 34%. The single biggest cause and entirely a cadence problem, not a product problem. Offering a four week interval before cancelling saves about a third of these.
- "Too expensive" — 21%. Mostly shipping, not coffee, which is a packaging and rate question for [[Supplier Directory]].
- "Wanted variety" — 18%. These people were on default [[House Blend Ember]] and never tried [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]].
- Moved away, or no reason given — the rest.

What reduces churn. A cadence change offer at cancellation. Rotating single origins for anyone who has taken three shipments. Collection in store instead of shipping, which also drives a café visit and a pastry from [[Pastry Partnership]].

The win-back sequence runs from the list in [[Email Newsletter Strategy]] and recovers roughly one in five lapsed subscribers, usually with a note about a new lot rather than a discount, per [[Pricing Strategy]].

Subscribers are disproportionately drawn from [[Loyalty Program]] members at fifty drinks, and from the conversations at the [[Farmers Market Booth]].
