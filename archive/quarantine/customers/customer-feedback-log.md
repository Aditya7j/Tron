# Customer Feedback Log

Everything a customer says twice gets written down. Reviewed monthly, and it is the least glamorous, most useful document we keep.

Recurring themes, ranked by frequency
1. Noise at peak. The room is hard-surfaced and loud from 7:30 to 9. Third most common complaint and the one we have done least about.
2. The queue at 8am. Addressed partly with the double points hour in [[Loyalty Program]] to shift demand earlier.
3. [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] as espresso. Roughly one in four sends it back. This produced the standing rule to offer a taste first, now in [[Barista Training Plan]].
4. Wanting oat milk at no upcharge. We hold the upcharge, per [[Pricing Strategy]].
5. Not enough seating with power outlets for the afternoon laptop crowd.

Two spikes worth remembering. Three weeks of complaints after the 11% price increase in year two. Two weeks of complaints about the blend tasting wrong during a Colombia stockout, traceable to [[Green Bean Inventory]] and to improvising [[House Blend Ember]] instead of telling people.

Praise clusters almost entirely on staff by name, which is the strongest argument for the investment in [[Barista Training Plan]] and the retention approach in [[Payroll and Tips Policy]].

Themes here feed [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]] decisions and the churn reasons in [[Subscription Churn Notes]].
