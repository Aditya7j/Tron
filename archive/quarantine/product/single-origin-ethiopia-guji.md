# Single Origin Ethiopia Guji

Washed Ethiopia from the Guji zone, 1,950-2,100 metres, heirloom varietals, sourced through Meridian Trading in the [[Supplier Directory]]. Cupped at 87.5.

Roast: light, dropped at 396°F, no development past 18%. Roasted first on Friday when the drum is cleanest, per the [[Roasting Schedule]].

Tasting: jasmine, bergamot, white peach, a clean tea-like body. Brews beautifully as pourover and V60, which is why it anchors the filter menu. As espresso it is polarising — bright and floral, and roughly one in four customers sends it back. Baristas should offer a taste before selling it as a shot, a rule that came directly out of complaints in [[Customer Feedback Log]].

This is our highest-margin bag at $22 for 12oz and also our most seasonal risk: the lot is finite and there is no substitute mid-year. When it runs out we do not fake it with another Ethiopian, we tell people, which is the practice defended in [[Local Press Outreach]].

It is the coffee that gets written about, features in most of the [[Instagram Content Plan]] posts, and pulls new subscribers more than anything else we sell. It also contributes 10% of [[House Blend Ember]].
