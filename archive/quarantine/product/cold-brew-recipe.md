# Cold Brew Recipe

Cold brew is 18% of summer revenue and nearly zero labour once the batch is built, which makes it the best margin item on the menu.

Concentrate recipe: 1kg coarse ground Brazil Cerrado to 8 litres cold filtered water. Steep 18 hours at cellar temperature, never in the walk-in — cold steeping under 50°F strips the sweetness. Filter through the cloth sock, then a paper polish. Yield is about 7 litres of concentrate.

Service: cut 1:1 with water over ice for the tap pour, 1:1.5 for the canned wholesale format. Nitro tap runs at 32 psi on beer gas. Keg lasts three days in summer, five in winter, and gets dumped at day six regardless.

Use the Brazil, not the blend. We tested [[House Blend Ember]] in cold brew and the Ethiopian component turned sour by hour twelve. Green stock for this comes off the Brazil line in [[Green Bean Inventory]].

Batch cadence sits on the [[Roasting Schedule]] as coarse grind Thursday, and the tap check is step five of the [[Daily Opening Checklist]].

Margin detail is worked out in [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]]: roughly 82% gross on the tap pour, our single best number, which is why the canned version is a candidate for [[Wholesale Accounts]] expansion.
