# Daily Opening Checklist

Opening barista arrives at 5:40am. Café doors open at 6:30am sharp — the commuter rush between 7:10 and 8:45 is roughly 40% of weekday transactions, so nothing on this list can slide.

1. Unlock, lights on, music at volume 4 (quiet until 7am).
2. Fire up the La Marzocco. It needs 25 minutes to reach stable brew temperature — start it before anything else. See [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]].
3. Pull and dump two shots, then dial in the current espresso. Target 18g in, 36g out, 27-30 seconds. If the beans are less than 4 days off roast, expect to grind coarser and check the [[Roasting Schedule]] for the batch date.
4. Brew the first two batches of filter coffee and log the recipe card.
5. Check the cold brew tap pressure and top off the keg. Recipe lives in [[Cold Brew Recipe]].
6. Receive the pastry delivery at 6:05am and count it against the invoice — details in [[Pastry Partnership]].
7. Count the till to the $250 float and note any variance in the ops book.
8. Wipe the bar, fill the milk fridge, restock cups and lids.

Anything broken, short, or weird goes in the ops book AND a message to the shift lead. Recurring issues get pulled into [[Staff Scheduling Rules]] so we stop staffing around a problem instead of fixing it.
