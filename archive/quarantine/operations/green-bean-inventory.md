# Green Bean Inventory

Green coffee is our single largest asset and the easiest thing to quietly waste. Count it every Monday morning before the week's plan is set.

Current standing lots:
- Ethiopia Guji, washed, 6 bags (60kg each) — the star of [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]]. Fast mover, reorder at 3 bags.
- Colombia Huila, 9 bags — the base of [[House Blend Ember]]. Reorder at 5 bags.
- Brazil Cerrado natural, 7 bags — blend body and the cold brew base. Reorder at 4 bags.
- Sumatra Mandheling, 2 bags — winter only, do not reorder until October.
- Swiss Water decaf, 3 bags — wholesale demand only.

Storage: pallets off the floor, 60-70°F, under 60% humidity, away from the roaster exhaust wall. Green coffee holds well for about nine months from harvest arrival, after which it tastes flat and papery no matter how well we roast it.

Two failure modes have actually cost us money. First, over-buying a seasonal lot we could not sell before it faded. Second, running out of Colombia mid-week and improvising the blend, which produced two weeks of customer complaints logged in [[Customer Feedback Log]].

Orders go through the vetted list in [[Supplier Directory]], and every purchase order gets matched to the value shown in [[Monthly PnL Summary]].
