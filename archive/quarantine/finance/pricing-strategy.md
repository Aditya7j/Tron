# Pricing Strategy

We are the second most expensive coffee within a mile and intend to stay there. Cheapest is a race we lose on volume; most expensive invites a comparison we cannot always win. Second is comfortable — the positioning mapped in [[Competitor Landscape]].

Current prices: drip $3.50, cortado $4.25, latte $5.25, pourover $6.00, cold brew $5.50, 12oz [[House Blend Ember]] $18, 12oz [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] $22.

Principles we hold to:
- Raise prices annually by a small amount rather than rarely by a large one. A 4% increase produced no measurable complaint; the 11% jump in year two produced three weeks of them in [[Customer Feedback Log]].
- Never discount the coffee. Reward frequency instead through [[Loyalty Program]], and never lead an email with a promotion, per [[Email Newsletter Strategy]].
- Price single origins to their true cost including shrinkage from the [[Roasting Schedule]], not to match the blend.
- Wholesale pricing is a separate ladder based on volume and delivery cost, held in [[Wholesale Accounts]].

Every price change is modelled against [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]] with a 32% ceiling on goods, and the impact lands in [[Monthly PnL Summary]] within two months.

When green coffee costs jump, per [[Green Bean Inventory]], we absorb one season and then reprice. We do not shrink the bag.
