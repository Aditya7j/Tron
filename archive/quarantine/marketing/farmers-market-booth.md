# Farmers Market Booth

Saturday market on Hawthorne, 8am to 1pm, April through October. Two staff, a folding table, a grinder, a hand-pour setup, and about 60 bags of coffee.

Direct economics are mediocre. Average market Saturday grosses $780 with roughly $310 of staff and stall cost against it. As a sales channel it barely clears the bar set in [[Monthly PnL Summary]].

As an acquisition channel it is the best thing we do. Roughly 30 loyalty signups per market, tracked with a market-specific code from [[Loyalty Program]], and those signups retain better than Instagram signups because they have already tasted the coffee and met a human.

What sells: 12oz bags of [[House Blend Ember]] to people who have never heard of us, and [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] to the ones who ask a second question. Brewed samples in small cups are non-negotiable — no samples, half the sales.

Bring the cupping notes and let people taste two coffees side by side. That conversation is the same one that works on the pourover flight in [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]].

Staffing comes out of the Saturday roster and must not thin the café bar during its own rush — the constraint written into [[Staff Scheduling Rules]].

It is also where two of our three [[Wholesale Accounts]] first found us, and where the reporter behind [[Local Press Outreach]] first tried the coffee.
