# Local Press Outreach

Three pieces of coverage in three years, all of them from relationships rather than pitches.

What worked. A food writer for the weekly tried our coffee at the [[Farmers Market Booth]], asked about the sourcing behind [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]], and wrote 800 words about traceability. That piece produced a two-week traffic spike and, more usefully, two of our current [[Wholesale Accounts]].

What did not work. A press release. A paid listicle placement. A paid influencer visit that produced one story and no measurable visits, an experiment we will not repeat and which is noted against the marketing line in [[Monthly PnL Summary]].

The angle that gets written about is always specific and slightly awkward: we do not bake our own pastry and we say why, per [[Pastry Partnership]]. We tell people when a lot runs out instead of substituting it, per [[Green Bean Inventory]]. We only roast twice a week, per [[Roasting Schedule]]. Specificity is the story.

Standing practice: when a journalist walks in, they get a seat, a taste, and twenty minutes, no matter how busy the bar is. Shift leads know to page the owner — the exception written into [[Staff Scheduling Rules]].

Coverage gets amplified once through [[Instagram Content Plan]] and once in [[Email Newsletter Strategy]], then left alone.
