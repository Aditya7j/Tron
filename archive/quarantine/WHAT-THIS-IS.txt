WHAT THIS IS, AND WHY IT IS NOT INDEXED
=======================================

Moved here Monday 28 September 2026, under PART 8 of the section 27 mandate.

The thirty .md files under this folder are the DEMONSTRATION CORPUS this console was built
and tested against: a coffee business - roasting schedules, a wholesale book, a pricing
strategy, a payroll policy. Thirty notes across seven folders. None of it is the employer's.
Not one fact in any of these files came from him.

They were useful and they are not thrown away. A harness that needed a question with a real
answer asked one of these; the galaxy needed enough stars to prove a layout; the retrieval
threshold was tuned against them. They are kept for exactly that, and they are kept READABLE:
a text editor opens them, and nothing here is encrypted, compressed or renamed.

WHY "UNINDEXED" IS THE WHOLE POINT
---------------------------------
The assistant answers questions about "your notes" and puts a CITATION under the answer. A
citation is the strongest claim this machine makes: it says a passage exists, in his
collection, saying this. If these files stay reachable, that claim becomes false thirty times
over - it cites a coffee business he does not own, in a voice that says "your notes", and it
cites it accurately, which is what makes it hard to catch.

Moving them out of notes/ was not enough on its own, and this is worth writing down because
it is not obvious: archive/ IS INDEXED FOR VECTORS. That is deliberate - the archive is the
filing cabinet, where a scanned contract nobody will ever wikilink can still be found. So a
file parked in archive/ stops being a star in the galaxy and stays fully searchable and fully
citable. Half a quarantine.

The folder name is what does it. "quarantine" is in SKIP_DIRS in BOTH build.py (the graph) and
ingest.py (the vectors) - two separate lists, for two separate walkers, and the first attempt
put the name in only one of them. The result was the bad case in its purest form: the galaxy
correctly stopped showing these notes while the brain went on citing all thirty of them.

HOW TO CHECK IT IS STILL TRUE
-----------------------------
    python build.py
      -> "vectors : 9 files" - his three notes and the six documents in archive/samples.
         Not 39. If a quarantined file appears in that log as "+ ... 1 chunk", the law broke.

    ask the assistant: "what do my notes say about coffee churn"
      -> it should say the shelf is bare. On 28 September 2026 it answered:
         "Nothing in your collection touches on coffee churn, Addi - the shelf is bare on
         that one." No citations, no search, no invention.

HOW TO PUT ONE BACK
-------------------
Move the file into notes/ under whatever folder it belongs in and run build.py. Nothing else
is needed; there is no list of quarantined files anywhere to keep in step. That is why this
was done by folder name rather than by a manifest.

DO NOT re-file any of it as his. The rule these were in the way of is the plain one: no
personal fact enters any note except through the Census, the remember hand, or the Scribe's
minutes. A coffee business that is not his is thirty notes' worth of exactly that.
