DEPOT HANDBOOK
==============

Opening. The northern depot opens at six and closes at eight, seven days a week. The southern depot is weekdays only, seven until five.

Keys. Depot keys are held in the wall safe by the loading bay. The combination is changed every quarter and is never written down in this handbook.

Fuel cards. One card per driver, never per vehicle, and never shared. A lost card is reported the same day.

Deliveries. Green bean sacks are checked for moisture and weight at the bay before the driver leaves, not afterwards.
