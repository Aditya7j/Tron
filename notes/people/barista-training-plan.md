# Barista Training Plan

Four weeks to the bar alone. Nobody works a rush unsupervised before week three, no exceptions, no matter how experienced the hire.

Week one — the room. Open and close, the whole [[Daily Opening Checklist]], the full clean-down in [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]], the till, the pastry count from [[Pastry Partnership]]. Tasting: our full menu, including everything on [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]].

Week two — coffee. Dialling in to 18g in, 36g out, 27-30 seconds. Grinding by scale, not feel. What [[House Blend Ember]] should taste like and what it tastes like when it is stale or under-rested, cross-referenced to the [[Roasting Schedule]]. Learn twenty regulars by name, per [[Regulars and Community]]. Learn to offer a taste of [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] before selling it as espresso — the rule that came out of [[Customer Feedback Log]].

Week three — milk and pace. Texture, temperature, latte art to a drinkable standard. Working a rush with a partner on bar.

Week four — judgement. When to remake a drink without being asked, when to comp, when to page the shift lead, and the press exception in [[Local Press Outreach]].

Sign-off is by a shift lead, not by hours served. Pay moves at sign-off under [[Payroll and Tips Policy]], and the roster in [[Staff Scheduling Rules]] assumes trained staff on every shift.
