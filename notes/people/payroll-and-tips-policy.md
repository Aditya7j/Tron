# Payroll and Tips Policy

Base pay starts at $19 an hour and moves to $21 at sign-off under [[Barista Training Plan]], then $23 for shift leads. Roaster is salaried. Everything above minimum is a retention decision, not a generosity decision.

Tips are pooled and split by hours worked, including the roaster and the person who worked the [[Farmers Market Booth]]. Card tips are paid out with payroll, cash weekly. Pooling is not up for debate — it stops the bar competing for the register and it is why service holds during a rush, per [[Staff Scheduling Rules]].

Tips average $4.10 an hour, so effective pay is roughly $23 to $27. That is the number that competes with the shops in [[Competitor Landscape]].

Referral bonus of $200 after a new hire's ninetieth day, which is the cheapest recruiting channel we have, per [[Hiring Pipeline]].

The economics. Labour at 37% of revenue in [[Monthly PnL Summary]] looks high against industry benchmarks. Replacing a barista costs about $2,400 in training time and lost speed, and customers name staff in almost all the praise recorded in [[Customer Feedback Log]]. Paying above market is cheaper than churning, and the regulars in [[Regulars and Community]] are attached to faces.

Any wage change is modelled before a price change, not after, per [[Pricing Strategy]].
