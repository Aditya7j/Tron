# Quarterly OKRs

Three objectives, no more. Anything not on this page is maintenance.

Objective 1 — make wholesale the stable half of the business.
- Grow [[Wholesale Accounts]] from $12,600 to $16,000 a month.
- Land two accounts, at least one from the canned product in [[Cold Brew Recipe]].
- Zero consistency incidents, meaning zero improvised blends of [[House Blend Ember]] during a stockout, which requires the reorder discipline in [[Green Bean Inventory]].

Objective 2 — stop leaking subscribers.
- Cut monthly churn from 6.5% to 4.5%, per [[Subscription Churn Notes]].
- Offer a cadence change at every cancellation.
- Put a rotating single origin, usually [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]], in front of every subscriber past their third shipment via [[Email Newsletter Strategy]].

Objective 3 — hold margin without raising prices twice.
- Labour to 35% of revenue in [[Monthly PnL Summary]] through the roster discipline in [[Staff Scheduling Rules]], without cutting the two-on-bar rule.
- Pastry waste under 8%, per [[Pastry Partnership]].
- Cost of goods under 30%, per [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]].

Explicitly not this quarter: a second site. The trigger conditions live in [[Second Location Analysis]] and none of them are met.
