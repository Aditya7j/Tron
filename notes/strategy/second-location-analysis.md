# Second Location Analysis

The question we keep circling. Honest answer today: not yet, and here is what would change it.

The case for. The Loring has headroom — about 96kg of green a week across two roast days per [[Equipment Loan Terms]] — so a second café would be supplied by an asset we already own. Brand awareness from [[Instagram Content Plan]] and [[Local Press Outreach]] extends past the neighbourhood we actually trade in.

The case against, in order of seriousness.
1. People. A second bar needs a shift lead we do not have and a hiring pipeline running at roughly double its current rate, per [[Hiring Pipeline]] and [[Barista Training Plan]].
2. Cash. Net is about $6,000 a month in [[Monthly PnL Summary]]. Build-out is $180,000 to $240,000 and both existing notes are personally guaranteed.
3. The thing that makes this shop work is not replicable. Half of café revenue comes from 180 people who know the staff by name, per [[Regulars and Community]].

Cheaper growth paths that should be exhausted first: more [[Wholesale Accounts]], canned cold brew from [[Cold Brew Recipe]], and growing the subscription book by fixing the cadence problem in [[Subscription Churn Notes]].

Trigger conditions: two consecutive quarters of net above $9,000, a trained shift lead in place, and wholesale above $18,000 a month. Tracked in [[Quarterly OKRs]].
