# Instagram Content Plan

Instagram is our only real acquisition channel and it works because it looks like a coffee bar, not an ad agency. Four posts a week, shot on a phone, natural light only, no stock imagery ever.

The rotation:
- Monday: process. Roasting, cupping, a scale, hands. The [[Roasting Schedule]] is quietly the best content generator we have.
- Wednesday: people. A barista, a regular, the Ivy Street baker from [[Pastry Partnership]]. Faces outperform latte art by roughly three to one on saves.
- Friday: product. Whatever just came off the roaster, usually [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] or a bag of [[House Blend Ember]].
- Sunday: place. The room, the light at 7am, the queue.

Stories daily, unpolished, with the menu board and the pastry case. Reels twice a month on anything with motion — the nitro pour from [[Cold Brew Recipe]] is the highest performing clip we have ever made.

What does not work: discount posts, hashtag stuffing, reposting other people's coffee photos, anything about pumpkin.

Launches are coordinated with [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]] and echoed in [[Email Newsletter Strategy]] two days later so the same news gets two shots.

Measurement is honest: saves and DMs, not likes. New follower to first purchase is roughly 6%, tracked through codes in [[Loyalty Program]].
