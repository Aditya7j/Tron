# Email Newsletter Strategy

One email every two weeks, Thursday 9am, plain text with one photo. 2,900 subscribers, 41% open rate, 4% click rate. The list comes almost entirely from [[Loyalty Program]] signups.

Structure that earns the open:
1. One paragraph of something true and specific — a lot arriving, a batch that surprised us, a staffing change.
2. One thing to buy, with a link. Usually the current [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] or a subscription.
3. One thing happening — a cupping, a menu change from [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]], the [[Farmers Market Booth]] schedule.

Email drives subscriptions better than any other channel. It also saves them: the win-back sequence described in [[Subscription Churn Notes]] runs from this list and recovers roughly one in five lapsed subscribers.

Rules learned the hard way. No more than two emails a month or unsubscribes spike. Never lead with a discount, per [[Pricing Strategy]]. Never send the same day as an [[Instagram Content Plan]] launch post — two days later performs measurably better.

Wholesale partners get a separate quarterly note about upcoming lots and price changes, which belongs with [[Wholesale Accounts]] rather than in the consumer list.

The best performing email we have ever sent was an apology for running out of a lot from [[Green Bean Inventory]].
