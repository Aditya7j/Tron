# Loyalty Program

Digital card in the point of sale, no app to download. Ten drinks earns one free drink of equal or lesser value. 1,340 active members, defined as at least one visit in 60 days.

What the data says. Members visit 2.4 times a week against 1.3 for non-members and spend 22% more per ticket, mostly because they attach a pastry from [[Pastry Partnership]]. The free drink costs us about $0.71 in goods, which is trivial against the visit frequency it buys — the arithmetic is in [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]].

Bonus mechanics we actually use:
- Double points before 7:30am, to thicken the early rush described in [[Daily Opening Checklist]].
- Bonus points for trying two new drinks at a menu launch, per [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]].
- A free bag of [[House Blend Ember]] at the fiftieth drink, which converts café customers into at-home customers.

Signup codes from [[Instagram Content Plan]] and the [[Farmers Market Booth]] are tagged separately so we know which channel actually produces repeat visitors rather than one-time traffic.

The program is also our only clean customer list, which makes it the backbone of [[Email Newsletter Strategy]] and the early warning system for the patterns in [[Subscription Churn Notes]].

Do not discount. Reward frequency instead — the position argued in [[Pricing Strategy]].
