# Monthly PnL Summary

Trailing twelve month averages, rounded, so we are arguing about the same numbers.

Revenue, $61,400 a month
- Café, $38,900
- Wholesale, $12,600 — see [[Wholesale Accounts]]
- Retail bags and subscription, $7,100
- Farmers market, $2,800 — see [[Farmers Market Booth]]

Costs
- Cost of goods, $17,800, about 29% of revenue. Detail in [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]].
- Labour, $22,500, about 37%. Driven by [[Staff Scheduling Rules]] and [[Payroll and Tips Policy]].
- Rent and utilities, $8,900.
- Equipment loan, $1,240 under [[Equipment Loan Terms]].
- Marketing, $900, mostly print and market stall fees.
- Everything else, $4,100.

Net, roughly $6,000 a month, or 9.8%. Good for a café, thin for anything with ambitions.

The two numbers that move net most: labour percentage, and pastry attach rate from [[Pastry Partnership]] which lifts average ticket from $6.10 to $9.40. Cold brew margin from [[Cold Brew Recipe]] is the best line item we have.

A closed day costs about $900 in café revenue, which is the argument for the service cadence in [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]].

Any expansion case, including [[Second Location Analysis]], starts from this page and the targets in [[Quarterly OKRs]].
