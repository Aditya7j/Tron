# Cost of Goods Breakdown

Blended cost of goods runs 29% of revenue, with a hard ceiling of 32% set in [[Pricing Strategy]].

By line
- Espresso drinks: 18-21%. Milk is the swing factor, not coffee. A 12oz latte carries about $0.34 of [[House Blend Ember]] and $0.52 of dairy.
- Drip and pourover: 12-15%. Best coffee margin in the building.
- Cold brew on tap: about 18% cost, 82% gross. See [[Cold Brew Recipe]].
- Retail bags: 42-46%. Thin, but it buys at-home habit and feeds the subscription tracked in [[Subscription Churn Notes]].
- Pastry: 45% purchase cost from [[Pastry Partnership]], plus waste. At 8% waste the category clears; at 11% it does not.
- Wholesale: 52-58% depending on tier, per [[Wholesale Accounts]].

Hidden costs people forget. Roast shrinkage of 14-16% from the [[Roasting Schedule]] means a 60kg bag in [[Green Bean Inventory]] yields about 51kg of sellable coffee. Free loyalty drinks from [[Loyalty Program]] cost $0.71 each. Dialling-in waste is roughly 300g of coffee a day, mostly at open, per [[Daily Opening Checklist]].

Cups, lids, and printed bags from [[Supplier Directory]] add about 4 points to retail cost and are the quietest inflation we have seen.

All of it rolls up into [[Monthly PnL Summary]].
