# Equipment Loan Terms

Two financed assets, both on the books, both nearly paid down.

La Marzocco Linea PB, bought used in year one for $14,500. Five year note at 7.9%, $293 a month, 14 payments remaining. This is the machine whose care is documented in [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]] and whose failure closes the café.

Loring 12kg roaster, bought in year two for $52,000 with a $12,000 deposit. Seven year equipment note at 6.4%, $947 a month, 41 payments remaining. It is the asset that made [[Wholesale Accounts]] possible and it sets the ceiling on how much we can roast per week under the [[Roasting Schedule]].

Combined $1,240 a month, carried as a line in [[Monthly PnL Summary]].

Terms worth remembering. No prepayment penalty on either note, so surplus cash can retire the espresso machine early. Both notes are personally guaranteed, which is the real constraint on any expansion in [[Second Location Analysis]] — a second lease plus a third note is the point where a bad quarter becomes a personal problem.

The roaster is insured for replacement value, the espresso machine for depreciated value, which is a mistake we should fix at renewal.

Capacity note: the Loring runs about 96kg of green a week across two roast days before we would need a third, which is the constraint behind the growth targets in [[Quarterly OKRs]].
