# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Target B2B micro-niches defined by high subscription price tolerance ($200–$300/month) rather than consumer markets, as businesses demonstrate significantly higher willingness to pay and lower churn friction for simple, value-dense tools.
- Adopt a "smart copy" strategy that combines validated concepts from existing successful platforms (found via Indie Hackers with $3k–$50k MRR or Acquire.com listings) and applies them to specific, narrow sub-niches to create new products quickly without reinventing the wheel.
- Automate product development by building a reusable "macro" or boilerplate framework that pre-integrs essential infrastructure like authentication, payments, and email, allowing you to rapidly launch multiple ideas so that 59 concepts can be tested to find the few (5–10%) that survive.
