# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Prioritize markets with proven, successful competitors over empty ones, as a lack of established players often signals a broken demand signal rather than an uncontested opportunity.
- Validate your go-to-market channel and ability to generate attention before writing any code, ensuring you can reach the specific market without relying on building the product first.
- Gate your product development on solutions to problems that are both urgent (requiring immediate resolution) and important (resulting in negative consequences if left unsolved) to avoid building features that users do not value highly enough to pay for.
