# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Prioritize a simple, direct, and concise cold email structure that clearly states who you are, why you're calling, and what meeting you want, rejecting both overly long, multi-paragraph sales enablement templates and fake-personalized "LinkedIn guru" scripts as neither scales nor persuades in real-world outreach.
- Focus 80% to 90% of your operational effort on scaling infrastructure—such as batching lists, automating tasks, and sending 100+ emails per day including follow-ups—rather than solely optimizing the wording, because the repeatable execution system is the primary driver of booking 3-5 meetings weekly.
- Implement a short, hard-hitting follow-up cadence that makes a concise point early and often within the first four or five touches, rather than using soft, vanilla, or overly stretched-out sequences, to signal value as a resource and force a decision to respond or be removed from the pipeline.
