# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Use live-scraped, verified contact databases for independent dental practices instead of resold lists, as this prevents email bounces and ensures outreach reaches genuine decision-makers at independent clinics.
- Structure daily outreach to exceed 100 total emails (including follow-ups) by prioritizing system automation and batching over manual crafting, since approximately 80-90% of cold email success depends on execution volume and workflow rather than message content.
- Reject both verbose, multi-paragraph sales enablement templates and "fake personalization" trends, instead sending direct, concise messages that clearly state identity, purpose, and a specific meeting request to avoid appearing manipulative or overly complicated to prospects.
