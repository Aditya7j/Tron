# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Adopt a high-volume outreach strategy targeting 100+ emails and follow-ups per day rather than the 15-20 typical of most reps, as approximately 80-90% of cold email success derives from operational scaling and repeatability rather than the message content alone.
- Avoid both overly complex, multi-paragraph "sales psychology" emails and short, "fake personalized" LinkedIn-style templates by using a direct approach that immediately states who you are, why you are calling, and the specific value you provide.
- Compress your follow-up cadence to hit prospects "early and often" rather than stretching it over 15 touchpoints, ensuring every message is concise and explicitly focused on booking the meeting instead of soft exploration.
