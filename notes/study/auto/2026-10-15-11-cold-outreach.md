# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Adopt a three-sentence, single-screen structure containing who you are, your specific relevance to their pain, and an assumptive call to action that proposes exact times rather than asking if the topic is worth exploring.
- Shift your outreach metric from meeting count to response volume, treating any reply including "no" or objections as a successful conversion for your initial contact but eliminating passive language that signals uncertainty.
- You must reach a volume of 100+ emails per day, including follow-ups, to achieve meaningful compounding results, as the "perfect" high-quality email approach takes too long per prospect to scale without a repeatable system.
