# Finance - auto-studied

- Establishing a consistent, paid salary—even at a "bare minimum" level that justifies your standard of living—creates the financial discipline and motivation necessary to sustain the business, avoiding the trap of personal financial distress disguised as noble sacrifice.
- Your true net worth often resides in illiquid equity rather than cash, meaning you must structure personal compensation independently of company valuation to protect your lifestyle from the reality that most private company shares are not liquid or may crash.
- As a founder, you must transparently communicate that your income is variable and tied to business performance, ensuring your family aligns with these financial realities rather than expecting the consistency of a traditional corporate salary.
