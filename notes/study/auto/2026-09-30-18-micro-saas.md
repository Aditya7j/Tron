# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Prioritize markets where competitors already exist because a lack of competition usually signals that potential customers have tried and failed to solve the problem, whereas crowded niches validate that an urgent, important, and revenue-generating demand is present.
- Validate your go-to-market strategy by assuming the product already exists to test if you can capture attention and distribution, ensuring that sales channels and audience trust are established before you invest significant time or capital into development.
- Challenge the instinct to charge 50-200% less than your product is worth, as underpriced micro-SaaS offerings often fail to signal the value density required to justify a subscription, directly undermining the revenue thresholds needed for sustainable growth.
