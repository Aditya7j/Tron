# Creator Economics - auto-studied

- Instagram offers no native ad-revenue share or per-view payout program, forcing creators to rely on high-margin, self-directed revenue streams like affiliate and subscriptions rather than platform-sponsored income.
- Subscriptions and direct-to-fan offers, such as Patreon or channel memberships, provide a recurring monthly revenue stream that decouples income from the volatility of ad performance or viral traffic spikes.
- Brand partnerships and affiliate marketing are the primary high-ticket income drivers in an environment where platforms like Instagram do not compensate for raw view counts, making audience trust and conversion capability the essential monetization levers.
