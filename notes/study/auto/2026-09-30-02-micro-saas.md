# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Before spending time on code, target a market that exhibits all three pillars of a viable micro-saas: an urgent and important problem, and the presence of existing successful competitors, because markets with zero competition often indicate that previous attempts failed due to a flawed market fit rather than a lack of awareness.
- Adopt a "distribution-first" validation strategy by assuming your product is already built to test if you can capture attention and generate leads, which protects you from the common pitfall of wasting months building a high-quality product for an audience you cannot efficiently reach.
- Structure your pricing based on the value density of your specific niche, as micro-saas products thrive when simplicity and focused value justify premium pricing relative to complexity, enabling you to reach sustainable recurring revenue (like $10k MRR) with fewer customers rather than competing on low volume.
