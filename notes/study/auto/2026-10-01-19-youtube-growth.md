# Youtube Growth - auto-studied

- Your audience effectively acts as your algorithm, so prioritize creating "boring-free" video topics over production polish, as a weak idea will fail to earn views regardless of thumbnail quality or editing.
- Use the first 30 seconds as a critical retention filter to prevent viewer drop-off, which directly suppresses YouTube's distribution of your content to new audiences.
- Target audience retention percentages relative to video length, aiming for over 40% on 5-6 minute videos or at least 30% on 15-20 minute videos, to secure the algorithmic pushes necessary for scalable, passive viewership.
