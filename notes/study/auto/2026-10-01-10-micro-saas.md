# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Validate your market by targeting a niche with an urgent, important problem and existing successful competitors, as a lack of competition typically signals a failed market rather than an opportunity.
- Prove distribution and lead generation for your target audience before writing any code, ensuring you can acquire customers at a reasonable cost (e.g., 175% ROAS on $5K ad spend) rather than relying on the product itself for validation.
- Anchor your pricing to your customer acquisition math by using a flat-rate or tiered structure that allows you to model the specific number of customers (e.g., 200 at $50/month) required to hit your minimum viable recurring revenue targets.
