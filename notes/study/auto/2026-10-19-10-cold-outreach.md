# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Adopt a direct, concise cold-email structure that explicitly states who you are, what you do, and the purpose of the meeting to bypass the "soft" follow-up cadences that fail to convert high-intention prospects.
- Prioritize outbound volume of 100+ emails per day (including follow-ups) over perfect personalization, as 80-90% of cold-email success is driven by scalable batching, automation, and tool usage rather than the message content itself.
- Implement a short, "hit them early and often" follow-up cadence of four to five touches to maintain visibility without triggering the fatigue associated with the ineffective 15-touch sequences often seen in standard sales training.
