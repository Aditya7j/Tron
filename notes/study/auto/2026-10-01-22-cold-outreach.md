# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Focus 80-90% of your effort on high-volume execution (100+ emails/day including follow-ups) and repeatable operational structure rather than perfecting copy, as the mechanics of scaling outreach drive success far more than the message text itself. - Abandon generalized social proof and fake personalization in favor of a direct structure that immediately states who you are, why you are contacting them, and what specific outcome you want, avoiding the "goofy" or overly soft approaches that fail to convert in B2B contexts. - Keep follow-up cadences short (4-5 emails) and frequent rather than stretching them over weeks, as concisely hitting the buyer early and often maintains presence without triggering the dismissal associated with long, vanilla sequences.
