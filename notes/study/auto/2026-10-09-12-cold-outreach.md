# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- 80 to 90% of cold email success hinges on execution outside the message itself, such as automation and batching, so you must prioritize building a repeatable infrastructure that scales to 100+ daily touches rather than obsessing over copywriting.
- Overly personalized "LinkedIn guru" tactics like fake observations are recognized as manipulative and ineffective, whereas the high-performing middle ground requires a direct, concise structure that clearly states who you are, why you are calling, and what you want without fluff.
- Standard sales enablement cadences fail because they are too long and soft, so the winning follow-up strategy is to "hit them hard and often" with concise messages focused on booking the meeting, then promptly move on once you hit 4 to 5 emails.
