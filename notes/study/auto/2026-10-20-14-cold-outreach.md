# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Prioritize volume and repeatability over craft, as 80-90% of cold email success derives from batching buyers, automating tasks, and sending 100+ daily touches (including follow-ups) rather than perfecting the text of any single message.
- Adopt a direct, concise structure that explicitly states who you are, why you are contacting them, and the specific next step, rejecting both verbose "sales enablement" playbooks and manipulative "LinkedIn guru" personalization hacks that trigger buyer fatigue.
- Implement short, hard-hitting follow-up cadences that are aggressive in frequency rather than length, moving past the contact after 4-5 concise attempts instead of stretching out 15-touchpoint sequences with soft, ambiguous calls to action.
