# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Prioritize volume and process over copywriting, as 80 to 90% of cold email success comes from executing 100+ daily touches and automation rather than perfecting the message structure.
- Reject both overly long "sales enablement" emails and gimmicky "LinkedIn guru" personalization, instead using a direct, concise format that immediately states who you are, why you are calling, and what you want.
- Implement a short, aggressive follow-up cadence that hits early and often to secure the meeting, because soft, low-pressure inquiries (e.g., "worth a chat?") and long 15-touch sequences fail to convert to booked calls.
