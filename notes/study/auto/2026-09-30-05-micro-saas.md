# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Start by selecting a market with proven, successful competitors and urgent problems, because markets lacking competitors are typically traps where previous attempts failed rather than prime opportunities for dominance.
- Validate your go-to-market strategy and ability to acquire attention before writing code, as ensuring you can reach your target audience is the critical lever that prevents wasting months building a product you cannot distribute.
- Implement pricing structures based on high value-density and simplicity, utilizing models like flat-rate or tiered plans to charge a premium in niche markets where your focused tool solves a specific urgent problem.
