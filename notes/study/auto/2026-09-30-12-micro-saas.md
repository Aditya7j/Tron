# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Choose markets with existing successful competitors to validate that the problem is urgent and commercially viable, avoiding undifferentiated ideas where the lack of competition signals a non-existent market.
- Validate your ability to acquire attention and distribution for your audience before writing code, ensuring you can reach the customers before investing months in building the product itself.
- Prioritize solving problems that are both urgent and important to the customer, ensuring they need a solution immediately and face significant negative consequences if they do not resolve it.
