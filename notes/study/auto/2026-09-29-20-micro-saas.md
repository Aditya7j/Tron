# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Prioritize markets with existing successful competitors over empty niches, as a lack of competition often signals a non-viable market where others have already tried and failed.
- Validate that you can acquire attention and distribution to the audience before building the product, ensuring they are reachable and willing to listen rather than asking them to buy unproven software.
- Select target problems that are both urgent and important to ensure customers are motivated to purchase immediately rather than deferring a solution that is merely helpful but not critical.
