# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Prioritize scaling to over 100 daily emails (including follow-ups) over crafting perfect individual messages, since 80-90% of cold email success depends on volume and repetitive tool usage rather than copywriting alone.
- Avoid both "sales enablement" traps (long, multi-paragraph emails) and "LinkedIn guru" traps (fake personalization like shared hobbies), as both are either ignored due to length or rejected as manipulative by modern buyers.
- Structure follow-up cadences to be short, hard-hitting, and direct about booking the meeting rather than asking for permission to explore, ensuring you "hit them early and often" to move past non-responsive leads quickly.
