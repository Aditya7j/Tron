# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Prioritize niche markets containing an urgent, important, and expensive-to-solve problem where existing companies are already successfully making money, rather than avoiding competition, because the absence of competitors often signals a fundamentally flawed market where past attempts failed to find buyers.
- Validate your distribution channels and ability to acquire the first 100 customers before writing a single line of code by assuming the product is already built, ensuring you are not spending months building a solution to a market you cannot actually reach or generate attention in.
- Calculate your churn-adjusted revenue requirements upfront to determine how many customers are needed to hit your income target, using benchmarks like $19/mo with 4% monthly churn to model whether a specific pricing tier realistically supports a sustainable, passive revenue stream.
