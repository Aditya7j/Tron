# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- The initial pitch rarely closes deals; 70 to 80% of meetings booked from email originate from follow-ups, so build a sequence where brief 24- to 48-hour follow-ups actively redirect attention to the first message rather than signaling an automated monthly cadence.
- Structure a high-velocity cadence of four emails across a seven-to-eight-day window interspersed with calls on the off days, as extending beyond five touch points significantly devalues your credibility and wastes time better spent reaching fresh prospects.
- Create hyper-specific templates targeted at a single role and industry (e.g., banking controllers) to standardize the message content, allowing you to send 100 personalized emails in an hour by changing only the name and company placeholders.
