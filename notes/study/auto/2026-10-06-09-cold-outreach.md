# Cold-Outreach - auto-studied

- Send 100+ emails daily, counting follow-ups, rather than relying on weekly LinkedIn "hacks" or low-volume, high-effort personalization that limits your output to 15–20 emails and fails at scale.
- Replace long, five-paragraph sales-enablement templates with short, direct, and professional messages that state your purpose and value immediately, as verbose or cheesy (e.g., "dog dad") attempts undermine credibility and get ignored.
- Redirect your effort from crafting the initial pitch to optimizing the 80–90% of success factors outside the email, such as batch-building targeted buyer lists, automating tasks, and executing concise, hard-hitting follow-ups that hit early and often.
