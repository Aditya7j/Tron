# Finance - auto-studied

- Allocate your personal capital to low-cost index funds that outperform active management, converting your founder equity into a reliable asset class rather than relying solely on illiquid company shares for your net worth.
- Treat your salary as a necessary operational expense rather than a moral failing, ensuring you take enough to sustain your standard of living to avoid burnout or financial distress that would jeopardize the long-term value of your startup.
- Negotiate for performance-based compensation such as profit margins or equity appreciation instead of fixed raises or bonuses in the early days, aligning your income with the company's actual profitability to preserve cash for growth.
