# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- A market with zero existing competitors is a high-risk signal indicating past validation failures, so look for niches where successful companies already prove demand but are underserving specific needs like AI integration or mobile access.
- You can validate demand and identify a viable niche before writing code by treating your product as already built and testing if you can acquire attention and leads, which prevents wasting months developing a solution nobody will pay for.
- To secure client money quickly, prioritize identifying a target market where the problem is both urgent and important, as this urgency allows for premium pricing and faster sales compared to problems that are merely annoying.
