# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Validate a market by identifying an urgent, important problem where successful competitors already exist, as the absence of competition signals a proven failure rather than an open lane for your product.
- Prioritize proving you can acquire attention and distribution in that specific market before writing any code, ensuring you do not spend months building a product that no one will see or buy.
- Structure your micro-SaaS to solve a top-of-mind pain point with high stakes for the customer, which accelerates trust building because the immediate value proposition aligns with their existing business pressure.
