# Finance - auto-studied

- Treat your personal compensation as a strict financial line item tied to company cash flow rather than an afterthought, ensuring you allocate a predetermined percentage of profits or a fixed minimum salary to support yourself while the business scales.
- Execute a "lifestyle arbitrage" by systematically eliminating personal expenses (like hired help or large weekend expenditures) to align your breakeven point with early-stage startup revenue, preventing the need for personal loans or asset liquidation to fund the business.
- Stop treating your unliquidated equity stake as personal wealth; assume your net worth is zero until a liquidity event occurs, and structure your personal finances to survive this volatile valuation without relying on the company to cover your living costs during downturns.
