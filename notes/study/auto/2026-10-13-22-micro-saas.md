# Micro-Saas - auto-studied

- Validate your specific niche by confirming competitors already exist and are making money, as a lack of competition signals that the market is too small or the problem is not actually solvable.
- Prioritize building for a market where the problem is both urgent (can't wait) and important (severe negative consequences if unsolved) to justify the premium pricing required for high-ticket B2B retainers.
- Reverse-engineer your product idea by assuming the tool already exists and testing your ability to get attention and leads in that specific market before writing a single line of code.
