# House Blend Ember

Our signature blend and about 55% of everything we roast. If a customer only ever buys one bag from us, it is this one.

Recipe: 60% Colombia Huila (washed), 30% Brazil Cerrado (natural), 10% Ethiopia Guji (washed). Medium roast, dropped at 410°F, 16% shrinkage. Tastes like milk chocolate, toasted almond, dried cherry on the finish. Built to hold up in milk without disappearing.

Why it works commercially: the Colombia and Brazil are volume lots with stable pricing from [[Supplier Directory]], so the blend cost stays predictable even when specialty prices spike. The 10% Ethiopia is the aroma and the story — it is also the component most at risk when the lot in [[Green Bean Inventory]] runs low.

It is the espresso in every milk drink, the wholesale default in [[Wholesale Accounts]], the anchor of the 12oz retail bag, and the default in the subscription discussed in [[Subscription Churn Notes]].

Rest window: four to twelve days off roast, per the [[Roasting Schedule]]. Under four days it gasses out and channels; past two weeks it goes flat and the cherry note vanishes.

Do not reformulate this quietly. Two changes in three years, both cupped blind against the old recipe and both announced to wholesale partners first.
