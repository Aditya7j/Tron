# Seasonal Menu Autumn

Autumn menu runs September 15 through November 30. The goal is a reason for regulars to change their order, not novelty for its own sake.

On the board:
- Maple Cortado — house maple syrup, 2oz espresso, 2oz milk. $5.25. Made with [[House Blend Ember]].
- Spiced Apple Shrub Soda — non-coffee, non-dairy, sells surprisingly well to the afternoon laptop crowd.
- Guji Pourover flight — three brew ratios of [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]] side by side, $12, our best educational sell.
- Sumatra filter returning in November when the winter lot lands per [[Green Bean Inventory]].

We are dropping the pumpkin latte. It sold, but it sold instead of espresso drinks and it needed a syrup we do not otherwise use. Cannibalisation without margin gain — the reasoning is spelled out in [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]].

Printed bags and cup sleeves need a 6 week lead time from Northline in [[Supplier Directory]], so artwork is locked by August 1.

Launch is coordinated with the [[Instagram Content Plan]] and one push in [[Email Newsletter Strategy]]. Bonus points are offered through [[Loyalty Program]] for trying two of the three new drinks.

Baristas taste everything on the board before launch day — a rule in [[Barista Training Plan]].
