# Pastry Partnership

We do not bake. We tried for four months in year two and it cost us a staff member and a lot of goodwill. Ivy Street Bakery, three blocks away, now supplies everything in the case.

Terms: we buy wholesale at 45% of our retail price, delivered daily at 6:05am, and we carry the waste. Order is placed the previous day by 2pm based on the shift lead's read of the week. Standing order is 24 croissants, 12 almond, 18 morning buns, 10 gluten free, plus a rotating tart.

Waste is the whole game. Above 8% waste and the category stops making money. We hit 11% in the first quarter of the deal because we ordered for the weekend we wanted, not the Tuesday we had. Now the order follows the traffic pattern recorded in [[Staff Scheduling Rules]] and the numbers land in [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]].

Counting the delivery against the invoice is step six of the [[Daily Opening Checklist]]. Shorted items happen about twice a month and Ivy Street credits them without argument.

Pastry pulls average ticket from $6.10 to $9.40 when it attaches, which is the most valuable single fact in [[Monthly PnL Summary]]. Baristas are trained to offer it, never to push it — see [[Barista Training Plan]].
