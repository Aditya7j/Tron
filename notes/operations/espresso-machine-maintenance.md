# Espresso Machine Maintenance

The La Marzocco Linea PB is a three-group machine bought used in year one, financed under the terms in [[Equipment Loan Terms]]. It is the most expensive thing in the building and the fastest way to lose a day of trade.

Daily, closing shift: backflush every group with water, then with detergent, brush the group heads, soak baskets and portafilters, wipe steam wands and purge them. Grinder hopper emptied and wiped Sunday.

Weekly: descale check on the water softener, drain tray deep clean, replace the shower screens if they are pitted, calibrate the grinder against a scale rather than by feel.

Quarterly: technician visit. Gaskets, valves, pressure calibration, water filter cartridge. Book it, do not wait for a symptom.

Symptoms and what they usually mean:
- Shots running fast and sour with no grind change: worn burrs or stale beans, cross-check the [[Roasting Schedule]] batch date.
- Group leaking at the edge: gasket, not technique.
- Steam pressure dropping mid-rush: descale overdue.

Every barista learns this list in week one of the [[Barista Training Plan]]. Downtime costs roughly $900 in lost daily revenue against the numbers in [[Monthly PnL Summary]], which is more than any service visit.
