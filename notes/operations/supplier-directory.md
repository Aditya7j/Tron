# Supplier Directory

Vetted suppliers only. A new supplier needs a sample, a cupping score, and a reference before it gets a purchase order.

Green coffee
- Cascade Importers (Portland) — our main book. Spot lots, 60kg bags, three day delivery, net 30. Best relationship, worst website.
- Meridian Trading — direct trade Ethiopian and Kenyan lots, higher price, better traceability, the source of [[Single Origin Ethiopia Guji]].
- Del Valle Brokers — Colombian and Brazilian volume for [[House Blend Ember]]. Sharp pricing on 10+ bag orders.

Dairy and alternatives
- Fern Hollow Dairy — whole and oat, Monday and Thursday delivery, non-negotiable on price but never short.

Packaging
- Northline Packaging — valved 12oz bags, cups, lids. 6 week lead time on printed bags, so plan around [[Seasonal Menu Autumn]] launches.

Pastry
- Ivy Street Bakery — see [[Pastry Partnership]] for the revenue split.

Payment terms drive cash flow more than headline price. Net 30 with Cascade is worth more to us than a 3% discount for prepay, a point argued out in [[Monthly PnL Summary]] and reflected in [[Cost of Goods Breakdown]].
